Long ticket lists scroll smoothly, and their scrollbar stays put as more tickets load

Scroll as fast as you like through a backlog of hundreds of tickets: rows
are ready before they reach the screen, the next page loads ahead of you,
and the scrollbar no longer jumps as it arrives. Slower devices benefit most.
