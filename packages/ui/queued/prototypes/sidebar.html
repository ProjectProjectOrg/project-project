Rail and pinned shelf

Open tickets and sprints from the page and the row appears under “Pinned”, outlined, with its pin showing. Pin it to keep it. Dashboard sits at the top of the rail; it shows your pins from every project.

Rail: Dashboard, then one tile per project, then New project. Your avatar sits at the foot and opens the account menu. Names show as ink tooltips after 300ms.
Live row: the page you are on but haven't pinned, outlined rather than filled, so it reads as temporary. It replaces itself as you move and disappears when you leave for a non-ticket page.
Pin: the row turns into a normal pinned row in place, with a short accent flash where it lands. Hover a pinned row to unpin it.
