## Board

Drag a card from Todo or In progress towards In review or Done. As a developer the locked columns dim their cards and say why as soon as the drag starts; over them the edge turns dashed and the cursor shows the drop isn't possible. As a PM they take the drop and say you are the exception. Cards already in a locked column still reorder.
