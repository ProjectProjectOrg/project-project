## Usage

```tsx
import { Guide } from "@pp/ui/guide"

<Guide
  open={!seen && selected > 1}
  onClose={markSeen}
  steps={[
    {
      target: () => list.current?.querySelector("[data-row-selected]"),
      title: m.tickets_guide_bulk_title({ count: selected }),
      body: m.tickets_guide_bulk_body()
    }
  ]}
  labels={{
    done: m.tickets_guide_done(),
    next: m.common_guide_next(),
    back: m.common_guide_back(),
    skip: m.common_guide_skip(),
    progress: (step, total) => m.common_guide_progress({ step, total })
  }}
/>
```

| Prop | Type |
| --- | --- |
| `open` | whether it shows |
| `steps` | `{ target, title, body, side?, onShow? }[]`: one for a single guide, several for a series. `target` is a ref or a function that finds the part; `onShow` runs as the step comes up (open the page its part is on) |
| `step`, `onStepChange` | which step shows, for a series; Next and Back ask for the next one |
| `onClose` | `(reason: "done" \| "skip") => void`: the last step's button, or the ✕ (Escape too) |
| `labels` | `{ done, next, back, skip, progress(step, total) }`: the buttons, the ✕'s name and tooltip, and "2 of 3" |
| `className` | the card's width and padding (`w-80 p-4`) |

## Rules

### One thing, in place

A guide points at the real thing, on the real screen, at the moment it first applies: never a picture of it. The rest blurs and dims (`--guide-scrim`), and the spotlight gets the 2px `--ring` with 6px of room and 16px corners, so the eye goes straight to it.

### It fades in, never pops

The blur's radius grows from 0 to 3px with the tint over the morph (260ms), instead of appearing at full strength, and leaves the same way.

### The spotlight stays live, the rest doesn't close it

The overlay has a hole over the part, so you can try it right there. A click on the blur does nothing: a guide ends only through its own buttons, the last step's "Got it" or "Done", or the ✕ in the card's corner ("Skip the guide", with its tooltip). Escape is the keyboard's ✕. Focus goes to the card's main button and back where it was after.

### A series glides

The card is the tooltip host's, pinned to the step's part (like a hover card that stays). Next and Back move the spotlight and the card together on the morph: the hole and ring glide, the card glides and resizes, the title and the step count roll to their new text, the body cross-fades, and Back grows in from the second step. Keep a series to three or four steps.

### Across pages

A step whose part is on another page says how to get there in `onShow` (navigate, open a panel). Until the part is on screen the guide waits, blur and all, and then picks up there. Mount it above the pages and keep `step` where a page change keeps it.

### Once per person

The app remembers who has seen each guide and never shows it again. A guide that keeps coming back is noise.
