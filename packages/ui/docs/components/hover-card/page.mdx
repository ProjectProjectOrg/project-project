## Overview

On top, a live sub-ticket count: rest the pointer on it and its card opens after 400ms; move into the card and click a row. Below, the three kinds of content drawn open in place: menu rows, text, and a card that only shows.

## Usage

```tsx
import { HoverCard, HoverCardContent, HoverCardTrigger } from "@pp/ui/hover-card"

<HoverCard>
  <HoverCardTrigger
    openOnClick
    aria-haspopup="dialog"
    render={<Chip interaction="hover" />}
  >
    <ProgressCount done={1} total={3} />
  </HoverCardTrigger>
  <HoverCardContent className="w-72 p-1">
    <TicketLines>…</TicketLines>
  </HoverCardContent>
</HoverCard>
```

| Part | Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `HoverCardTrigger` | `render` | the trigger element (a link, a button, a router `Link`) | `<a>` |
| | `openOnClick` | `boolean`: also opens on click, for the keyboard and touch | `false` |
| `HoverCardContent` | `side` | `"top" \| "bottom" \| "left" \| "right"` | the container's (`HoverSide`), else `"bottom"` |
| | `align` | `"start" \| "center" \| "end"` | `"start"` |
| | `sideOffset` | `number` | `6` |
| | `className` | width and padding; `pointer-events-none` for a card that only shows | `px-2.5 py-2` |
| `HoverCardTitle` | `children` | the card's title, two lines at most | |
| `HoverCardFooter` | `children` | the last row under a hairline: an outcome or an action. It bleeds out of a `p-3` card | |

`closeHoverCard()` closes it at once, for a click on a row inside. A popover that opens on hover and only shows something (the ticket peek on a row's title, an image in an attachment chip) takes the same skin with `<PopoverContent surface="hover">`.

## Rules

### One side per container

Every hover in a container opens on the same side: in a row, a section header or a board card, tooltips and hover cards all open below (they wrap themselves in `<HoverSide side="bottom">`). Going from a tooltip above to a card below made the box jump across its trigger; on one side it glides. A tooltip or card that sets its own `side` keeps it, so set one only where its container doesn't; `TooltipGroup` sets the side for everything inside it too.

### A hover card, not a tooltip

A tooltip (`role="tooltip"`) describes its trigger: screen readers read it with the trigger and can't move into it, so it can never hold links. Anything you might click (sub-tickets, linked tickets, a tag's tickets) goes in a hover card, whose trigger stays a real link or button and whose content can be reached.

### One host for tooltips and cards

Hover cards are drawn by the tooltip's host, so one hover shows at a time, with one delay (400ms) and warm-up: moving from a tooltip to a card, or back, glides the box to its new place and size over 180ms while the corners ease from 9px to 14px and the card's content fades in. Its content renders where the `HoverCard` is written (into the host through a portal), so it keeps that spot's context. A hover trigger inside an open card doesn't take the host over; the card stays.

### What it keeps from Base UI

It replaced Base UI's Preview Card and keeps what that gave: it opens on hover after the delay and on keyboard focus, stays open while the pointer is on it (150ms to cross the gap) and when its trigger is pressed, and closes on Escape or a click outside. (A tooltip does close on a press: the press is what its label was for.) `openOnClick` opens it from the keyboard and touch and keeps it open until then. The card has no tooltip role and doesn't describe its trigger, which keeps its own name. Scrolling inside a card keeps it open; scrolling the page closes a hovered one.

### One family of hovers

It wears the tooltip's skin: the raised fill (`--tooltip`: white in light, a lifted grey in dark), the hairline and top light (`--tooltip-edge`), 14px squircle corners and no shadow. Opening and closing match the tooltip too: a fade and a scale from 96%, 160ms in and 120ms out, ease-out. Menus and click-opened popovers wear the same skin (`popupSurfaceClass`), so everything that floats is one family; a hover card differs only in its smaller corners and in opening on hover.

### A record: title, then footer

A card that reads like a record (a pull request, a tag) pads 12px (`p-3`), leads with `HoverCardTitle` and ends with `HoverCardFooter`: one row under a hairline, out to the card's edges, for the outcome (checks failing, merged two days ago) or the one action (filter by assignee). Don't hand-draw the hairline.

### Padding follows the content

Text gets 10px by 8px (the default). Lists of tickets are menu rows (`TicketLines`), so the card pads them 4px in (`p-1`) and the rows take the menu's highlight, which tells you they can be clicked. A card that only shows (git, the sprint estimate) sets `pointer-events-none`, so it never catches the pointer on its way past.
